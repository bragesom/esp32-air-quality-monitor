⚙️ Settings

The SCD30 measures at most every 1800 s (30 min). Shorter intervals write to the SD card more often.

🧪 Calibration

Off by default. ASC only works if the room reliably reaches fresh-air CO₂ (~400 ppm) — e.g. aired daily. Leave it off for a closed room.

Compensates the sensor's own self-heating (reported = measured − offset).

Put the sensor in stable outdoor air for at least 2 minutes, then calibrate to the reference (outdoor air ≈ 420 ppm).
